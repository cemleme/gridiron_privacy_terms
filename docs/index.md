## About the game

Gridiron Idle is an idle American football game. You build a program from the Backyard to the Hall of Fame, find the bottleneck in your offense, and fix it. Your team plays itself; you decide what to improve.

The game has **no accounts**, **no in-app purchases**, and plays **offline**. It shows optional rewarded video ads — you only see one if you choose to watch one. Your saved game stays on your device.
