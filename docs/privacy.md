# Gridiron Idle — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]
**Applies to:** the mobile game **Gridiron Idle** for iOS and Android
**Operator:** Gridiron Idle ("we", "us", "our")

Gridiron Idle is published by Gridiron Idle. This policy explains what information is handled when you play the game, who else is involved, and the choices you have. It is written to be read, not to be impressive.

**The short version.** Your game progress stays on your phone. We have no accounts and no servers that store your game. The game does show optional rewarded video ads, and the advertising technology used to do that collects some device and advertising information so it can work — that part is explained in full in section 5.

---

## 1. Information we collect

### 1.1 What we do not collect

We do not ask for, and the game does not have a way for you to give us:

- your name, email address, phone number, or postal address;
- an account, username, or password (the game has no sign-in);
- your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or files;
- your precise location.

The game has no text input fields at all, no chat, no messaging, no comments, and no way to upload or share content.

### 1.2 What stays on your device

To work, the game saves information **only in its private storage on your device**:

- **Your saved game** — your team, your players (whose names the game makes up itself), your money, rings, Playbook Points, yards, tier, records, achievements, and similar progress, plus the time you last saved so the game can work out what your team earned while you were away.
- **A backup copy of your save**, kept on the device so an accidental reset can be recovered.
- **Your settings** — sound and music on or off, number format, reduce-motion choice, and which kinds of notification you want.
- **Notification bookkeeping** — whether the game has already asked for notification permission, how many times you have opened the game, and when notifications were last scheduled, so the game can keep to its limit of one a day.

This information is not sent to us. **Settings → Reset Save** starts a new game. **Uninstalling the game removes all of it** from your device.

Your phone's operating system may include app data in device backups that you control (for example iCloud backup on iPhone, or Android's backup to your Google account). Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under your account and their privacy policies. We cannot see or access them.

### 1.3 Information collected by third-party services

The game includes third-party software that does transmit information off your device. This is described in sections 4 and 5.

We do not operate a server that receives, stores, or has access to your game progress.

---

## 2. Notifications

The game can remind you about things happening in the game — for example that your offline earnings are full, or that a championship is within reach. These are **local notifications**: the game schedules them on your device itself. No server sends them, and no information is sent anywhere to create them.

- The game asks for notification permission only after you have played for a while (after your first championship), never on first launch.
- If you do not allow notifications, the game works exactly the same without them.
- If you allow them, the game sends at most one a day. You can turn each kind off in the game's Settings, or turn all of them off in your phone's settings at any time.

## 3. Permissions the game requests

| Permission | Why | Can you refuse? |

| Notifications | Local game reminders (section 2) | Yes — the game works fully without it |
| App Tracking Transparency (iOS only) | To let the advertising service use your device's advertising identifier to measure and personalise ads (section 5) | Yes — you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you |

## 5. Advertising and tracking

### 5.1 What kind of advertising the game shows

The game shows **optional rewarded video ads only**. You will only ever see an ad if you choose to tap a button that offers one — for example a temporary cash boost, or unlocking a new pitch. There are:

- no banner ads;
- no interstitial ads;
- no app-open ads;
- no ads that appear on their own;
- no advertising in the game before you choose to watch one.

If you never tap an ad button, you will never see an ad.

### 5.2 What the advertising service collects

To serve, measure and pay for ads, Google Mobile Ads and RevenueCat may collect and process:

- your device's **advertising identifier** (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android);
- your **IP address**, which is used to determine your approximate (city-level) location for ad delivery;
- **advertising data** — which ads were loaded, shown, clicked, or closed;
- **app and device information** needed to deliver an ad, such as device type and operating system version;
- **diagnostic information**, such as crash and performance data related to the ad software;
- a **RevenueCat app identifier** used to attribute ad revenue.

### 5.3 Tracking across other apps and websites

**On iOS**, the game asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before the advertising identifier is used to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not available for that purpose, and you will see non-personalised ads instead.

**On Android**, the advertising ID is available to the advertising service. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Under the definitions used by Apple and Google, the processing described in this section counts as **tracking** on iOS and as **sharing data with third parties** on Google Play. We are describing it accurately rather than avoiding the label. You can limit it as set out in section 6.

### 5.4 What we receive

We receive **aggregated advertising reports** — totals such as how many ads were shown and how much revenue they generated. We do not receive information that identifies you, and we cannot look up an individual player.

## 6. Your choices and controls

| What you want | How to do it |
|---|---|
| Change your ad privacy choices | **In the game: Settings → Ad privacy choices.** This opens Google's consent form, where you can change or withdraw your choices for personalised advertising |
| Stop the game using your advertising identifier for tracking (iOS) | Decline the App Tracking Transparency prompt, or change it later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking |
| Reset or delete your advertising ID (Android) | Android Settings → Google → Ads |
| Stop local notifications | In the game's Settings, or in your phone's settings |
| Delete everything the game stores on your device | **Settings → Reset Save**, or simply uninstall the game |
| See what we have about you | We hold no personal information about you, so there is nothing for us to look up |

## 7. Children

Gridiron Idle is a game for a general audience. It **is not directed at children**, and it is not part of Apple's Kids category or Google Play's Designed for Families programme.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game has no accounts, no chat, no messaging, no content uploads, and no way for a child to give us information. The app's store listings are rated for a general audience.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us at **[CONTACT EMAIL]** and we will help.

Because the game shows advertising, we recommend that parents review their device's ad-personalisation settings, as described in section 6.

- **By the advertising services:** Google and RevenueCat keep information according to their own retention policies. We do not control those periods and cannot delete information held by them; the controls in section 6 are the way to limit their use of your advertising identifier.
